Carrborobotics — a competitive robotics team focused on designing, fabricating, and programming robots for regional and national competitions.

### Key Features

- End-to-end robot design from CAD modeling through fabrication and assembly.
- Custom motor driver and control electronics for drivetrain and actuator systems.
- Sensor integration for autonomous navigation and obstacle detection.
- Collaborative team environment with hands-on mechanical and electrical engineering.
