## Overview

A complete teardown, restoration, and mechanical rebuild of a Creality CR-10 large-format ($300 \times 300 \times 400\,\text{mm}$) 3D printer. The project focused on diagnosing motion inconsistencies, rebuilding the mechanical frame, overhauling the extrusion and thermal subsystems, tuning firmware motion parameters, and restoring the machine to dependable operating condition for ongoing engineering prototyping.

### Video 1: Teardown & Mechanical Reassembly (Timelapse)
The workbench timelapse captures the comprehensive mechanical restoration process:
- Disassembling the X and Z axes to inspect aluminum V-slot extrusions and linear guide wheels.
- Tightening and truing the gantry to eliminate sag across the 300 mm span.
- Adjusting eccentric nuts to achieve smooth carriage travel without play or binding.
- Securing the diagonal frame support struts to minimize frame flexing during high-acceleration moves.
- Routing and securing motor wiring harnesses, endstop switches, and Bowden tubing.

### Video 2: Extrusion & Print Validation
Following mechanical assembly, the printer was brought online for thermal and motion verification:
- **Heated Bed & Hotend Calibration:** Tested thermal stability and ran PID auto-tuning to ensure tight temperature control.
- **First Layer Adhesion:** Verified 4-point manual bed leveling against the glass build surface with initial skirt extrusion.
- **Extrusion Consistency:** Monitored extruder stepper feed rate and nozzle flow to verify absence of clogs or skipped steps.

---

## Key Features & Upgrades

- **Frame Rigidity & Motion Alignment:** Squaring the aluminum V-slot frame with diagonal reinforcement braces to eliminate Z-wobble across the $400\,\text{mm}$ vertical height.
- **Extrusion & Hotend Refurbishment:** Rebuilt the hotend assembly with fresh PTFE Bowden guide tubing, new 0.4 mm brass nozzle, and re-torqued heater block to prevent filament oozing.
- **Firmware & Kinematics Tuning:** Configured stepper steps-per-mm, calibrated extrusion multiplier, and tuned acceleration and junction deviation values for cleaner surface finish.
- **Engineering Prototyping Utility:** Serves as a dedicated high-capacity additive manufacturing platform for custom avionics enclosures, test fixture brackets, and mechanical mockups.
