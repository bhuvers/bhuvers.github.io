Hey, I'm Bhuvanesh! I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Georgia Institute of Technology with a passion for building things that work at the hardware level.

My interests span **PCB Design**, **Power Electronics**, and **Analog Design/Circuitry**. I love the challenge of taking a circuit from schematic to a finished board — designing layouts, optimizing power delivery, and working through the intricacies of analog signal chains.

During my time at GT, you'll see me working on Avionics (harnessing, board bringup and development) for the Yellow Jacket Space Program, working on Analog Electronics (FPAAs) in Dr. Hasler's ICE Lab, or working on audio electronics for Dr. Lanterman. Outside of that, I'm an avid basketball and baseball enthusiast (my favorite player is Aaron Judge!).

Feel free to check out my [projects](/projects/) or [experience](/cv/) to learn more about what I've been working on, or reach out via email or LinkedIn.
